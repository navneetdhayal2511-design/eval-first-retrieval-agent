# Time away from work

Time away is requested by the employee and recorded by the manager. This chapter does not describe on-call pay.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. Requests longer than 3 consecutive days must be filed in Ripple HR at least 14 days before the first day off.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of sick leave each calendar year. Sick leave does not roll over.

## Bereavement

Bereavement leave is 5 paid days for the death of a spouse, child, parent, or sibling. The manager records bereavement in Ripple HR.
