# Incident response

Incidents use the severity definitions from the on-call chapter. This chapter says who communicates and when the write-up is due.

## Incident bridge

For a Sev-1, the incident commander opens the bridge within 10 minutes of the page.

## Status page

During a Sev-1, the communications lead updates the public status page every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved.

## Postmortem

A blameless postmortem is published within 5 business days after a Sev-1. The postmortem lists the timeline, the impact, and the corrective actions.
