# Hardware bay safety

This handbook covers leave, production reviews, and the hours teams spend on incidents. The chapters below are the source of truth for the hardware bay.

## Eye protection

Everyone in the hardware bay wears sealed eye protection, including visitors. The bay attendant can refuse entry when eye protection is missing.

## Lithium batteries

Lithium battery packs are stored only in fire cabinet B2. The cabinet must stay between 15 and 25 degrees Celsius. Packs with swollen cells are moved to the quarantine shelf and are not shipped.

## Emergency line

The hardware emergency line is 415-555-0148. For a battery fire, leave the bay and pull the hallway alarm. Do not use water on lithium battery fires.

## Visitors

Visitors in the hardware bay need a full-time employee escort. Escorts sign the paper log at the bay door.
